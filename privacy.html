Privacy

Your trips, kept private.

This page explains what information LovinglyLost uses, where it's kept, who else is involved, and how to delete it. If anything here is unclear, get in touch.

Last updated November 17, 2026

The short version

  • You can plan trips without an account. Nothing about those trips is saved on our side.
  • Your live location during a drive stays on your phone. It isn't saved or sent to LovinglyLost.
  • If you make an account, only you can see what's saved in it.
  • No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your information.
  • You can delete your account, and everything in it, from the account page at any time.

When you plan a trip

To find places, plan your route and look for stops along the way, the places you type in and the area of the map you're looking at are sent to Mapbox, the mapping service LovinglyLost uses. Trips planned without an account aren't saved anywhere by LovinglyLost.

When you tap Start Drive, your phone or browser asks for permission to use your location. It's used only on your device, to show where you are and your next turn. LovinglyLost doesn't save it or send it anywhere. Mapbox still loads the map pictures for the area around you, the same way it does whenever you look at a map.

Links that open a stop in Google Maps or Apple Maps take you to those apps, and their own privacy policies apply there.

Saved on your device

LovinglyLost remembers a few things in your browser's own storage, so the app works the way you left it. This stays on your device and isn't used for tracking or ads:

  • Your color theme, the stop types you picked, and your road preferences
  • Whether you've seen the welcome animation, the tour and the latest update
  • That you're signed in, if you are
  • A trip you were planning when you went to sign in, kept for up to 24 hours
  • Copies of the app's files and Mapbox's list of place types, so it opens quickly and works offline

Clearing your browser's data for this site removes all of it.

If you make an account

Accounts are optional. If you make one, these are kept with Supabase, the service that runs LovinglyLost accounts:

  • Your email address and password. Your password is stored scrambled (hashed), so no one at LovinglyLost can see it.
  • The details you add on your account page: your name, home town and "a little about you"
  • Your road preferences and color theme
  • Your saved trips (their starting point, stops and days), favorite stops and places to avoid

Security rules on each table mean only you can see or change what's in your account. Supabase also sends the emails for confirming your account, resetting your password and changing your email.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information to anyone.
  • We don't show ads, and there are no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We don't use analytics services that follow you around the web.
  • We don't share your information, except with the services below that make the app work, or if the law requires it.

How long it's kept, and deleting it

What's in your account stays until you remove it. You can change or delete anything on your account page, one item at a time.

Delete my account (under Sign-in & security on the account page) permanently removes your account and everything saved in it straight away. A copy may stay in the account service's routine backups for a short time before those backups are replaced.

If you write to us, we keep your message only as long as we need it to reply and to fix what you told us about.

Your choices

Wherever you live, you can ask us to show you the information in your account, correct it, send you a copy, or delete it. Most of this you can do yourself on the account page. For anything else, contact us and we'll reply as soon as we can, and within 30 days.

Some places, like California and the European Union, give you these rights by law. We honor them for everyone.

Children

You need to be 13 or older to make a LovinglyLost account. We don't knowingly keep information from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has made an account, let us know and we'll delete it.

Keeping it safe

LovinglyLost only connects over secure (HTTPS) connections, account data is protected by per-person security rules, and passwords are never stored in readable form. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Changes to this page

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date at the top. Big changes will also be announced in What's new.
