About

Getting lost, on purpose.

LovinglyLost is a road-trip planner for drives that are about more than getting there: the long way around, the overlook you'd have missed, the little winery an hour off the highway.

What it does

Tell it where you're starting and where you want to go, and it plans the drive and finds the pretty places along the way.

One route through every stop

Add as many stops as you like and get a single route that links them up in order.

Scenic stops along the way

Viewpoints, waterfalls, gardens, wineries and more, found near your route. Tap one to add it to the trip.

Turn-by-turn, with a heart

Start a drive and a little heart follows you on the map, with your next turn always on screen.

An afternoon or a whole week

Going from A to B, or taking your time? Split the driving across as many days as you like.

How it works

  1. 1Pick your starting point and the places you're headed.
  2. 2Choose the kinds of spots you love, and we'll look for them near your route.
  3. 3Hit Start Drive and go. Any stop can also open in Google Maps or Apple Maps if you'd rather use those.

A note on your privacy

Your live location is only used on your own phone, to show where you are and what turn is coming up. LovinglyLost doesn't save it or send it anywhere. The places you type in are sent to Mapbox, the mapping service that works out your route.
